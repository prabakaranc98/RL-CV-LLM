    A: \textcolor{ferndark}{Roger started with 5 balls. 2 cans of 3 tennis balls each is 6 tennis balls. 5 + 6 = 11.} The answer is 11.\\[4pt]
    Q: The cafeteria had 23 apples. If they used 20 to make lunch and bought 6 more, how many apples do they have?};
  \node[tag, anchor=south west] at ($(i2.south west)+(0,-0.55)$) {model output};
  \node[outp, accent, fill=ferntint, anchor=north west] (o2) at ($(i2.south west)+(0,-0.65)$) {A: \textcolor{ferndark}{The cafeteria had 23 apples originally. They used 20 to make lunch. So they had 23 - 20 = 3. They bought 6 more apples, so they have 3 + 6 = 9.} The answer is 9.};
  \node[tag, anchor=south east, text=ferndark] at ($(o2.north east)+(0,0.1)$) {right};
  \draw[flowa] (i2.south) -- ($(i2.south)+(0,-0.42)$);
  \end{scope}
  \node[dims, anchor=north west] at ($(o1.south west |- o2.south)+(0,-0.25)$) {green: the worked steps, shown in the example and then written by the model};
